# Gate 03F — Squeeze-pot River CBet

Status: **P-heavy policy boundary frozen**.

DeepCrusher has no dedicated deep-stack squeeze three-barrel tree. CashCrusher therefore carries Gate02 family 11 forward and preserves three current survivor origins: original opener, pre-3bet coldcaller that continued versus the squeeze, and post-3bet coldcaller.

The coldcaller-origin families are more selected than opener-call and receive no pure-air River baseline. The opener family may use only a narrow missed-premium-draw/high-pressure blocker bluff when Turn was already HU and Hero is IP. A multiway-Turn survivor never receives that bluff automatically.

Strong made value remains active; OP/strong TP require clean runouts and actual low/moderate River SPR more often than in SRP. Multiway squeeze Rivers are strongly value-heavy and use deepest-effective SPR for any field-wide low-SPR relaxation.

Natural low SPR in squeeze pots is retained as a strategic input. It does not convert TP+ into a universal stack-off class, and no old Spin commitment threshold is automatically deleted or imported.
